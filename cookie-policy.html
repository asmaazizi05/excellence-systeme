1. What Are Cookies?

A cookie is a small alphanumeric text file stored on your computer or mobile device by a website server when you visit. Cookies help the website recognize your device on subsequent visits, streamlining your navigation and ensuring smooth operation.

2. Why We Use Cookies

  • Maintain Preferences: Preserving your selected language (French or English) across pages.
  • Security & Anti-Abuse: Protecting estimation quote forms against automated bot submissions.
  • Performance Metrics: Gathering aggregated, anonymized insights to continually enhance site navigation speed and content clarity.

3. Categories of Cookies Used

A. Strictly Necessary Technical Cookies

Essential for basic website functionality, form security, and responsive styling. The site cannot function properly without them.

B. Functional & Preference Cookies

Remember user choices such as language toggles (FR/EN) to deliver a consistent, localized browsing session.

C. Anonymized Analytical Cookies

Gather aggregated statistics without personal identification to monitor platform health and audience trends.

5. Consent & Legal Authority

Necessary technical cookies are exempt from prior consent pursuant to Moroccan Law No. 09-08 and international guidelines because they are essential for service delivery. You may block all cookies directly via your browser settings.

6. How to Configure Your Browser

You can manage and block cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Block all cookies.
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions.

7. Consequences of Disabling Cookies

If you disable cookies entirely, some convenience features (such as persisting your English language choice across tabs) may reset upon refreshing. However, all technical guides, contact numbers, and project estimate forms remain fully accessible.
